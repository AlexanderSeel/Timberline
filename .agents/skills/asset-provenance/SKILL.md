# Asset intake
- Require exact source URL, author when known, license URL, check date, files and modifications in `assets/asset-manifest.json`.
- Poly Haven and ambientCG are CC0; other marketplaces and model packs must be verified individually. Free access is not necessarily redistribution permission.
- Tripo free vs paid commercial rights and asset ownership differ; check actual account tier and terms before any release.
- No copyrighted game screenshots as shipped art; build original Timberline identity.
- Scan downloaded archives and never execute bundled scripts by default. Separate Blender source and optimized GLBs.
